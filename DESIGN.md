# Kesari Ink (current, 17 Nov 2026)

The live direction, decided with Piyush after several rounds. **A professional company's B2B SaaS, not flashy.** It replaces the "The Sash" drafts below, which are kept for their reasoning.

- **Structure.** The landing page is only a landing page: a centred hero with the product window under it, a proof strip, three feature rows that each show a real piece of the product, a metrics band, the product grid, a comparison, a short FAQ and a call to action. Everything deeper has its own page: `/products/{assistant,train,verify,host,pay,devices}`, `/solutions`, `/developers`, `/pricing`, `/trust`, `/roadmap`. The header shows Products and Solutions menus.
- **Colour.** Nearest Pantone references:
  - white paper;
  - ink Pantone 7547 C (#131E29);
  - one accent, kesari (saffron) Pantone Orange 021 C (#FE5000), for actions and status only;
  - lines Pantone Cool Gray 1 C (#D9D9D6).
  - Text-sized orange is #B83A00, for contrast.
- **Type.** Satoshi for everything, headings included, at moderate B2B sizes. Zodiak only for figures (metrics, prices). Both are by the Indian Type Foundry, self-hosted from the official files (`src/app/fonts`, ITF Free Font License, credited in the footer).
- **Motion.** Only where it explains: the product window stepping through its screens, the relay hand-over, menus fading in, and small hover feedback. No reveals, count-ups, ribbons, textures, shimmer or pulsing. Reduced motion is respected.
- **Honesty.** Every product carries a status: live on testnet, being built or planned. Example data is labelled.

# The Sash

The design direction for ekido.duckdns.org, written before the 17 Nov 2026 redesign and revised the same day: the first draft (numbered legs, full-width bands, a scroll-drawn rail) read like scrolling through slides, so the page now works like a product site. When a design decision is hard, this page decides it; when it can't, add the missing sentence here first.

Ekido is a relay, and the page is the race. A visitor runs it from top to bottom, and the sash, the job itself, is passed from section to section. Each section is one leg. The design's job is to make that baton pass feel physical and inevitable, so that by the end the visitor has watched a job travel from a description to a model they own.

**Revised:** product first. A product nav with one primary button; a hero that shows the app itself (a live product window), not an illustration; then dense, flowing sections: a proof strip, a bento of features with small working UIs, use cases, device owners, an FAQ and a final call to action. Headings are smaller and the product does the talking. The original paragraph follows for the materials it still governs.

Space is editorial and asymmetric. Headlines are huge and sit on the left; content hangs off a strict grid instead of floating in centred boxes. Every leg carries an oversized outlined race-bib numeral (01 to 05). Paper legs alternate with full-width ink legs, bands rather than cards, so the page has a beat: paper, ink, paper.

The materials are paper (#FBF8F3) and ink (#1F2A33), both with a fine grain, and white cards with a warm, soft shadow so things have weight. Orange (#E8622C) is rationed. Its one big use is the sash: a single band at the logo's angle. Everywhere else it appears only as a small signal, like a kicker, an active state or a pass mark.

Type is Fraunces for display (opsz 72, SOFT 0, WONK 0, bold), set huge (about six times body size) and tight. IBM Plex Sans is for reading. IBM Plex Mono is for anything that is data: the Hinglish examples, step counts and results, shown as real artifacts (a chat bubble, an invoice, a JSON card) rather than described.

Motion is calm, with one orchestrated moment: the relay in the hero plays once, and as you scroll, the sash draws itself down the page from leg to leg. With reduced motion, the sash is simply drawn and nothing moves.

**Is not:** a SaaS template, crypto-neon glow, or sterile flat. Kills on sight: gradient blobs, glassmorphism, shimmer, identical card grids, everything fading up on scroll.

**Signature move (revised):** the logo's two bands, huge and at its angle, sweep behind the live product window in the hero, and return as the finish tape under the final call to action. Orange appears almost nowhere else.

Brand constants: ink #1F2A33, ink-2 #44525D, muted #6B7882, sash #E8622C, sash deep #B5461A, sash tint #FBEADF, sash soft #FFB48F, paper #FBF8F3, card #FFFFFF, line #E7E0D5; on ink: card #2A3843, line #3A4955, text #FBF8F3 / #D6DDE1.
